==================================================
  EMAILJS SETUP GUIDE — WonderVoyage Booking Emails
==================================================

STEP 1 — Create a FREE EmailJS Account
---------------------------------------
Go to: https://www.emailjs.com
Click "Sign Up Free" (200 emails/month free)


STEP 2 — Connect Your Email (e.g. Gmail)
------------------------------------------
Dashboard → Email Services → Add New Service
Select Gmail (or Outlook, Yahoo, etc.)
Follow the OAuth steps to connect your admin email account
Note your SERVICE ID (e.g. "service_abc123")


STEP 3 — Create an Email Template
------------------------------------
Dashboard → Email Templates → Create New Template

Set:
  To Email:   {{admin_email}}
  Reply To:   {{reply_to}}          ← so you can reply directly to guest!
  Subject:    🧳 New Booking Request [{{booking_ref}}] — {{tour_name}}

STEP 4 — Get Your Public Key
------------------------------
EmailJS Dashboard → Account → General → Public Key
(e.g. "AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOp")


STEP 5 — Update tours.js
--------------------------
Open: c:\Users\See Sight Tours\Desktop\claudedemo\tours.js
Find these 3 lines near the top (around line 265) and replace with your values:

  const EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY  = 'AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOp';     ← your Public Key
  const EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID  = 'service_abc123';         ← your Service ID
  const EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID = 'template_xyz789';        ← your Template ID
  const ADMIN_EMAIL         = 'you@youremail.com';      ← your admin email


STEP 6 — Test It!
------------------
Open tours.html → select a tour → pick a date → click Book This Tour
Fill in the guest form → Submit
Check your admin inbox — you should receive the full booking details email!
