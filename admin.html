Help

Setting availability defines when a tour departure is open for bookings. Select a product, set a date range, choose which days of the week depart, and define time slots per day.

The "Availability capacity" sets the maximum guests per time slot. Once bookings reach this limit, the slot automatically shows as Sold Out.

Each departure day can have multiple time slots (e.g. 08:00–17:00 and 14:00–23:00). Use "+ Add Slot" to add more times per day. Guests choose their preferred time when booking.

Check the days you want to apply the same schedule to, configure one day's time slots, then click "Copy to Selected Days" to apply it instantly to all checked days.

For assistance, refer to the setup guides in your setup/ folder, or contact your WonderVoyage administrator.
